NewPipe Material ye NewPipe ka foroko yɛrɛmahɔrɔnyalen ye Android kama, min sinsinnen bɛ Material 3 dilancogo kan, barokun kulɛriw fangama ni bololabaarakɛlaw, ani ɲɛnayeli polisicogo ɲuman k’a sɔrɔ a bɛ NewPipe dɔnniya dɔnta mara.

NewPipe Material bɛ fɛɛrɛ nɔgɔman, min bɛ mɔgɔ dogo, min bɛ ciyɛn sɔrɔ NewPipe fɛ, o mara. A tɛ plateforme jatebɔsɛbɛn wajibiya, a tɛ da Google framework libraries kan, wa a dabɔra ka baara kɛ ni Google Play Services tɛ.

Fɛn minnu bɛ ciyɛn sɔrɔ NewPipe fɛ olu ye:
- Wideyow ni ɲɛjirali ɲɛnamaw lajɛ ka bɔ baarakɛminɛnw na minnu bɛ dɛmɛ
- Background (Kɔnɔnafili) fɔli
- Popup (Popup) fɔli
- Sigida fɔlisenw
- Abonnés (abonnements) minnu bɛ kɛ k’a sɔrɔ i ma don plateforme (jɔyɔrɔ) jatebɔsɛbɛn dɔ kɔnɔ
- Kanal kuluw ni feeds
- Baarakɛminɛn minnu bɛ dɛmɛ sɔrɔ, olu ɲinini ni u lajɛ
- Wideyo kunnafoni, wideyow minnu bɛ tali kɛ o la, ani kuma fɔlenw yɔrɔ minnu na
- Wideyo, lamɛntaw ani sɛbɛnniw telesarse yɔrɔ minnu na
- App dataw donna ani k’u bila ka taa kɔkan walasa ka backup ni migration kɛ

NewPipe Material bɛ bi foroko identité fara a kan ani Material-focused polish:
- Material 3-inspired surfaces, dialogues, settings, tabw, ani navigatiɔn
- Dynamic Material You kulɛri dɛmɛni yɔrɔ min na a bɛ sɔrɔ
- Manuel theme kulɛri preset
- NewPipe Material app dantigɛli
- Ka foroko attribution jɛya
- Bɔli jɔli bolonɔbilalen don NewPipe Material kama
