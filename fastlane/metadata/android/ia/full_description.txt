NewPipe Material furca independentis NewPipe est pro Android, Material 3 in consilio, dynamico et manuali colore, et accurata expolitio visualis, servata experientia nota NewPipe.

NewPipe Material leve pondus servat, aditum hereditarium secreti-amicum ab NewPipe. Rationem suggestum non requirit, ab Google framework libraries non dependet, et sine Google Play Services ad operandum destinatur.

Lineamenta hereditaria ex NewPipe includunt:
- Watch videos and live streames from support services
- Background playback
- Popup playback
- Local playlists
- Subscriptiones sine signo ad rationem suggestum
- Channel coetus et alit
- Quaerere et percurrere officia suscepit
- Video singula, videos cognata, et commentarios ubi in promptu sunt
- Download video, audio, et captiones ubi in promptu sunt
- Import et export app notitia pro tergum et migratio

NewPipe Material addit modernam identitatem furca et nitorem materialem feruntur:
- Material 3 superficies, alternis, occasus, tabs et navigationes inspiratae
- Dynamic Material You color firmamentum ubi available
- Argumentum manuale color presets
- NewPipe Material app identitatis
- Patet furca attributionis
- Release builds signati pro NewPipe Material
